Research · Innovation · Sustainability

Hongrui (Joy) Jiao

Applied Economics · Environmental Economics · Regional Development

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Economics at China University of Geosciences (CUG), Wuhan, supervised by Professor Hongbing Deng, with expected completion in June 2028. From September 2025 to September 2026, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Padova under the supervision of Professor Roberto Antonietti, supported by the China Scholarship Council. I will return to Padova from January to July 2027 to continue this collaboration, supported by the IAERE 2026 Visiting Scholarship.

My research focuses on green innovation, the circular economy, and the geography of innovation. I examine how digital technologies, environmental policy, and collaboration networks shape innovation outcomes across firms and cities. My methodological interests include panel data analysis, spatial econometrics, and social network analysis.

Alongside quantitative research, I value insights from fieldwork. In 2022, I participated in the CUG Yangtze River Source Scientific Expedition on the Qinghai–Tibet Plateau, contributing to field research on ecological migration and regional sustainability.

Research interests: Circular economy; green innovation; innovation networks; twin transition; ESG.
