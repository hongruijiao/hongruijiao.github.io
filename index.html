Hongrui (Joy) Jiao

Applied Economics · Environmental Economics · Regional Development

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Economics at China University of Geosciences (CUG), Wuhan, supervised by Professor Hongbing Deng, with expected completion in 2028.06. From 2025.09 to 2026.09, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Padova under the supervision of Professor Roberto Antonietti. I will return to Padova from January to 2027.07 to continue this collaboration, supported by the IAERE 2026 Visiting Scholarship.

My research focuses on green innovation, the circular economy, and the geography of innovation. I examine how digital technologies, environmental policy, and collaboration networks shape innovation outcomes across firms and cities. My methodological interests include panel data analysis, spatial econometrics, and social network analysis.
